How it works

No copying text, no writing prompts. NextReply reads the screenshot you select and understands who said what.

1

Press the shortcut

From any app. The screen dims and you drag over the part of the chat you want help with.

2

Get three replies

A small card appears beside the chat with three different ways to answer — in the same language and tone as the conversation.

3

Click and paste

Your pick is copied to the clipboard and the card closes. Paste it into the chat, edit if you like, and send.

Three styles, every time

Short, natural, and written the way people actually text. It won't invent facts or promise things on your behalf.

Your chats stay yours

NextReply only looks at your screen when you press the shortcut, and only at the area you select.

  • Screenshots are kept in memory and never saved to disk — on your computer or on our servers.
  • We don't store your conversations or the replies we suggest. Our logs contain usage metadata only, such as time and duration.
  • Nothing runs in the background and nothing is sent automatically. You choose what to paste.
  • No access to your chat accounts, contacts or message history.

Questions

Which chat apps does it work with?

Any app you can see on screen — WeChat, WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord, Messenger, iMessage on the web, email threads and more. NextReply works from a screenshot, so it doesn't need to integrate with the app.

Which languages are supported?

Replies are written in the same language as the conversation, matching its tone and formality.

Do I need an account?

No. You can try it right after installing. Sign in with your email to get free daily replies and a sign-up bonus.

Does it send messages for me?

Never. It only copies the reply you click. You decide whether and what to send.

Which platforms are supported?

Windows 10 and 11 today. macOS is coming soon.
