Simple pricing

One credit is one set of three suggested replies. You're only charged when replies are generated — if a screenshot isn't a conversation, or there isn't enough context, it's free.

Billing questions

When do I get my monthly replies?

Each paid billing period adds that plan's replies to your account. Replies you don't use expire shortly after the period ends; they don't roll over.

Why is there a daily limit on paid plans?

It's a fair-use limit to keep the service fast for everyone and to protect your account if it's misused. Most people never reach it.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You keep your plan until the end of the current billing period, and you won't be charged again. See the refund policy.

How do I pay?

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
