Getting started

  1. Run the installer. NextReply starts in the system tray (bottom-right of the taskbar).
  2. Open any chat and press Ctrl + Shift + R.
  3. Drag over the conversation — or click a window to select all of it.
  4. Click the reply you like. It's copied; paste it with Ctrl + V. Press Esc at any time to cancel.

You can change the shortcut in Settings from the tray icon. Ctrl + Shift + R replaces the browser's hard-refresh shortcut while NextReply is running.

Free replies and signing in

You can try NextReply without an account. When the trial runs out, sign in with your email from Settings → Account to get free replies every day and a sign-up bonus. See pricing.

System requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
  • An internet connection — screenshots are analysed in the cloud and are never stored
