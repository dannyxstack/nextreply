The short version

  • We only see your screen when you press the shortcut, and only the area you select.
  • Screenshots are processed in memory to generate replies and are never saved — not on your computer, not on our servers.
  • We don't store your conversations or the replies we generate.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

Screenshots

When you select an area, the app sends that image over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards it to our AI provider, Anthropic, to read the conversation and write suggestions. Our server keeps the image only in memory for the duration of the request. We don't write it to disk or to logs, and we don't keep the generated replies.
