Cancelling

You can cancel a subscription at any time from your account page or the app (Settings → Account → Manage subscription). You keep your plan and remaining replies until the end of the current billing period, and you won't be charged again.

Refunds

  • If you were charged by mistake, or NextReply didn't work for you, contact us within 14 days of the charge and we'll refund it.
  • Otherwise, payments for a billing period that has started aren't refunded, except where the law requires it.
  • When a payment is refunded, the replies included in that billing period are removed from your account.

Free replies

Free, trial and bonus replies have no cash value and can't be refunded or exchanged.
